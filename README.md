# NCT Content Intelligence Dashboard

An AI-assisted, interactive newsroom analytics and content intelligence dashboard designed to analyse television programming, guest appearances, interviews, anchors, reporters and newsroom performance.

## 🌐 Live Website

[**Visit NCT Content Intelligence Dashboard**](https://nctanalysis.ai.studio/)

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Overview Dashboard:** Newsroom KPIs and performance insights.
- **Guest Analytics:** Guest appearances, frequency and duration.
- **Interview Analytics:** Interview tracking and programme analysis.
- **Anchor Analytics:** Anchor appearances and visibility.
- **Personality Analytics:** Tracking public personalities and political figures.
- **Weekend Programming:** Weekend programme analysis.
- **Special Programming:** Identification of special programming patterns.
- **Cross Analysis:** Comparative analysis across channels and programmes.
- **Interactive Filters:** Date, channel, language, programme, category and other filters.
- **Data Export:** Excel and CSV reporting capabilities.

Developed through AI-assisted programming and iterative development using Google Gemini AI Studio and ChatGPT, with a focus on interactive data analytics, newsroom intelligence and business intelligence visualisation.
